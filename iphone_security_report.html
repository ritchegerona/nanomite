6Recommendations & Remediation

R-01: Audit App Permissions

  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Security and review Camera, Microphone, Location Services, Contacts, and Photos access for each installed app.
  • Revoke permissions for any app that does not need them (especially the lesser-known apps listed in Section 4.2).

R-02: Remove Unrecognized Applications

  • Delete any app you did not intentionally install, focusing on the low-recognition and region-mismatched apps identified in this report.
  • Check Settings > General > iPhone Storage for large or unfamiliar apps.

R-03: Verify Pairing Records

  • Confirm that only your trusted computers appear in Settings > General > Reset > Location & Privacy > Reset Location & Privacy (this clears all trust pairings; re-trust only your own machines).
  • On each computer you trust, check ~/Library/Lockdown/ (macOS) or %ProgramData%\Apple\Lockdown\ (Windows) for unknown pairing records.

R-04: Maintain iOS Updates and Strong Authentication

  • Keep iOS updated to the latest minor release via Settings > General > Software Update.
  • Ensure a strong alphanumeric passcode is set (Settings > Face ID & Passcode).
  • Keep Find My / Activation Lock enabled.
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your Apple ID.

R-05: Consider Deeper Forensic Review (if high assurance is required)

  • If you suspect targeted surveillance, engage a mobile forensics provider for a Mobile Verification Toolkit (MVT) analysis or full filesystem extraction.
  • Capture network traffic while the device is idle and while using suspect apps to look for unexpected outbound connections.
