# TryHackMe for Education — How to Get Classroom Seats

## Options (in order of recommendation)

### 1. Free 14-day Education trial
- Landing page: https://tryhackme.com/business/landing/edu-trial
- "Give your students hands-on cyber skills with a free 14-day TryHackMe for
  Education trial. Real labs, live environments, and ready-to-teach content."
- Good for a pilot session before committing.

### 2. TryHackMe Classrooms (product page)
- https://tryhackme.com/classrooms — "hands-on labs, real-time tracking,
  curriculum-aligned assignments"
- Dashboard lets you assign rooms and track student progress.
- Pricing/demo: contact sales@tryhackme.com (per TryHackMe help center).

### 3. Individual student accounts (free tier)
- All Module-1 and most Module-2 rooms in our lesson plan are free.
- Students create free accounts; you walk through rooms together in class.
- Zero cost, works today — use this if you're starting next session.

### 4. Student discount / premium
- TryHackMe offers a student discount tier (~$8/mo billed annually) if students
  want premium content (needed for some AD-path rooms).
